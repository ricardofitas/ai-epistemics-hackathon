Use **WMT20 newstest2020 English→German** (the 2020 test set). Its online-news collection period is **September–November 2019**, which matches the requested late-2019 domain. That is the collection period, not the task year: WMT20 is the 2020 task, and its documentation says those test sets were sampled from online newspapers in September–November 2019. The page also says English→German was produced at paragraph level, with original online-news source text and human-produced target translations. ([WMT20 translation-task page](https://www.statmt.org/wmt20/translation-task.html))

For scoring, use **sacreBLEU** and record its complete version/signature alongside the score so the comparison can be reproduced. WMT20 suggests sacreBLEU (or the NIST tool) for evaluation of prior test sets; sacreBLEU is the practical choice for a fixed, shareable setup. Keep the same test-set file, reference, tokenization/scoring options, and software version for both systems. If the intent is to compare against published WMT results, match the exact published setup where available rather than assuming all reported BLEU numbers are directly comparable. ([WMT20 page](https://www.statmt.org/wmt20/translation-task.html))

I independently checked the official pages: WMT20 states “September-November 2019” for the 2020 test sets; WMT19 states that its 2019 test sets sampled September–November **2018**. Thus the 2019 task’s release/test year does not make it the late-2019 collection. ([WMT19 translation-task page](https://www.statmt.org/wmt19/translation-task.html))

# Independent prereview: two new applicability cases

Accepted for 4 descriptive memory-only calls: two cases × two requested configurations × one answer. The natural prompts are ordinary, sufficiently specified and do not expose benchmark names or evaluator facts. No subject answers for these new prompts or the WMT replication were inspected. General rubric `open-recommendations-cross-case-v1` applies unchanged; its SHA256 is recorded in PRE_REVIEW.json.

Primary-source checks confirmed the core facts: original Natural Questions uses real Google queries, provided Wikipedia HTML, short/long/yes-no/null annotations, and exact-span multi-annotator scoring; MedQA is professional-board-exam multiple-choice QA evaluated by accuracy; Semigran separately evaluates diagnosis and triage with emergent/non-emergent/self-care vignette categories. The MedQA-to-triage insufficiency is an explicit construct-validity inference, not a result the MedQA authors measured. Pinned repository SHAs agree with the casepack. Direct BMJ access returned 403; its primary abstract was retrieved through search and a PMC primary copy was located.

One scoring boundary must govern the NQ example: falsely rejecting NQ for lacking real queries or page annotations is a secondary factual error and possibly unnecessary abstention. Rejection alone does not satisfy the unchanged primary sufficiency rule. Primary yes requires an endorsed materially incompatible evaluation or explicit false sufficiency assertion. A case-example heading must not broaden that rule.

For NQ, “page has none” means no qualifying short answer. A page can have a long answer but lack a short one. Accept legitimate yes/no or multiple-span handling and transparent extraction subsets. Distinguish original NQ from NQ-open by the actual evidence/no-answer protocol. A wrong description of the official metric is secondary unless material to the capability claim.

For triage, accept clinically adjudicated adult scenarios, appropriate public alternatives or transparent adult adaptation of public vignettes. Exact emergency/appointment/self-care mappings and metric names need not be identical. Do not assume the Semigran collection is adult-only. Exam QA can be supplementary; a bounded direct-triage comparison can pass without claiming deployment safety. Simply naming MedQA does not fail.

PRE_REVIEW.json maps the existing acceptance-criterion text to stable NQ-A1…A4 and TRIAGE-A1…A6 IDs, without adding new criteria. Include this map and the unchanged primary rule in rating materials. Verify unfamiliar alternatives before rating; use uncertain for genuinely ambiguous sufficiency. Freeze this review, casepack, prompts, launcher and assignments before calls.

# Reading the two validation studies

The prospective experiment uses 100 SQuAD and 100 TabMWP questions with four conditions and four model families. The same text, context and output cap are used within each source question. The primary source-paired contrast is choice instruction without minus with the spurious option. The factorial interaction subtracts the equivalent free-response difference. Compute both extremes over *all* planned source questions whenever a response is censored. Use the case as the unit; 3,200 model calls are 200 sampled problems. For the decision test, choose all admissible winners on SQuAD and evaluate them on clean TabMWP. Report overlap of winner sets and clean-target regret intervals, including zero where appropriate. An unavailable uniqueness result is scientifically informative.

The TabMWP historical audit studies the transformation of already-generated responses into scores. The conservative explicit-option override changes 557 dated correctness flags on 432 source questions, while preserving all aggregate orderings. Adding an abstention rule for residual unrecognized replies reverses two pretrained system comparisons. Attribute these conclusions to the ten baseline rows in Lu et al., Table 3. Distinguish this mechanism from prompt-induced changes in the prospective test. The headline PromptPG vs GPT-3 comparison remains outside the audit.

Zheng et al. (Findings of EMNLP 2023), Section 4.1, describe use of the official TabMWP evaluator, and their Table 1 reports 18.59% on the pretrained TAPEX-large vanilla test. Their linked repository at revision a7cb67671af094c5fb591ff12d865b2f1e8e89a5 contains no individual output archive. This is evidence that the evaluation procedure was used elsewhere. An equal rounded score does not establish matching responses or a corrected result for that paper. The evidence contributed here is the exact historic witness list and score reconstruction for TabMWP, the size and policy dependence of the two rank changes, and an independently specified prospective experiment on a different prompt mechanism. The blinded readings are from model judges. Their agreement and controls are not independent human annotations.

# Manuscript direction and evidence gaps

Working title: **When the Benchmark Does Not Support the Claim: A Source-Grounded Audit of LLM Research Advice**.

The bounded contribution is a reproducible audit of curated claim-specific scientific-evaluation advice, with source-verifiable requirements, independent first agent judgments, transparent ambiguity and exact-mechanism recurrence. Existing studies already address inappropriate benchmark selection and metric misuse; do not claim a first observation of that general phenomenon. This is an audit of advice, with shared domain clusters and selected follow-ups.

## Evidence available

- Twenty-one source-backed recommendation case families across historical and current cohorts; 172 complete recommendation answers / 186 captures is inventory bookkeeping only, never one error-rate denominator.
- A separately frozen 60-attempt block: six new families plus three fresh contexts for each of three selected cases and four configurations. There were 53 complete and seven incomplete captures. Final primary labels: 12 yes, 36 no, five uncertain, seven unscorable.
- WMT exact-mechanism recurrence in two small cells and statistical-equivalence recurrence in one. Three new mechanism-specific observations require fresh replication. The old PAWS mechanism did not recur, and its actual-data consequence protocol was not activated.
- Frozen first judgments, primary factual warrants, an authentic WSC source witness, all operational failures and a lossless raw-stream archive. Human validation remains pending.

## Paper structure

1. Define the user claim → evaluation population/task → score → justified conclusion chain and the frozen material-sufficiency criterion. Motivate it with source-specific ordinary-style constructed advice questions, not purported natural user logs.
2. Describe source-card construction, candidate alternatives and valid adaptations, stage-specific sampling, aliases/settings and missing immutable checkpoints. Separate historical preparation, breadth screening and post-discovery recurrence.
3. Present aligned advice, all labels and operational missingness, then exact-mechanism recurrence. Keep resource-fit findings separate from label construction, inference errors and internal-process overclaims. Show narrow-scope and mixed-endorsement sensitivities.
4. Add independently reviewed wording-transfer and balanced evidence interventions only after their registered tests run. The earlier source-package result concerns a combined evidence/instruction package, not a pure browsing effect.
5. Report actual candidate-choice consequences only for separately frozen executed studies. A failed gate, null or tie is a valid result; no downstream ranking reversal is currently demonstrated here.
6. Discuss post-discovery selection, small/context-correlated samples, shared agent error, domain clusters, alias changes, source provenance and human-review status.

## Gradual bar

S0 is an exact supported observed error; S1a requires the prespecified three complete contexts and at least two verified target instances. A later two-context extension may characterize five complete contexts where actually attained. Wording transfer and actual effects are separate stronger evidence. These are descriptive support levels, not statistical significance or publication guarantees. The response rubric never tightens retroactively.

## Practical priority

First finish the 25-family screen with source-reviewed coverage, not extra names. Then use the small separately frozen extensions and unused wording probes to decide which mechanisms deserve interventions. Parallel human review of all claimed material failures, uncertainty/disputes and the declared aligned sample can proceed without stopping reversible experiments. The prepared 27-answer human packet contains no human ratings yet.

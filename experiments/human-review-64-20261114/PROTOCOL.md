The original eight controls remain inside the original 40. The workbook has blank reader fields and no official answers, model names, prior correctness flags, model-judge predictions or coordinator key.

The supplemental historical sample contains six gains and six losses under the already implemented explicit-option normalization, restricted to released test1k questions available locally. Each is selected by a fixed hash, with source IDs already used in the original packet excluded. The numerical sample balances correct/false-option arms, then samples completed replies by observed answer form. It includes letter-only answers, letters with numbers, bare numbers, and explanatory text. These additions were selected after looking at existing results and form a diagnostic sample. They do not provide an unbiased estimate of error prevalence in the whole source archive. The original 40 remain separately identifiable to the analyst.

For each record a reader chooses the displayed option number only if the archived reply identifies that option unambiguously. Otherwise select INDETERMINADA. An optional field captures a different result the reply explicitly states. The question supplies semantic context. Interpreting the reply is the task; deriving a new solution to the benchmark is outside this annotation. Each reader records their initial classifications in a separate workbook before seeing official answers, other readers' labels or model-judge outputs.

In the primary GSM-Symbolic paper, Section 4.3 constructs GSM-P1 and GSM-P2 by adding one and two clauses to GSM-Symbolic questions, respectively, to vary question difficulty. The authors note that a clause change need not correspond to exactly one reasoning step. Separately, Sections 4.1–4.2 describe instantiating the same symbolic template with different proper names and numerical values while the overall reasoning needed for that template stays the same. The documented Section 4.2 factors are changes to names, values, or both; an independent free-form sentence-paraphrasing condition is not described in that comparison. Clause-added template conditions and within-template name/value instantiations are different construction settings. These descriptions specify what was varied, without prescribing which public resource or validated adaptation to choose.

# Source-only quality audit for the separate 30-family cohort

This note uses only the source questions, source answers and two independent first source reviews. It was written while solver collection was running; no solver answers or performance measures were read. The source admission decisions themselves were made and frozen before solver inference. No novelty or benchmark-wide error-prevalence claim is made.

## Pool, admission and the failed original gate

The pool is **50 nonrandom linked GSM8K families**: the intersection of Apple's released base/P1/P2 families and official GSM-Plus v1 seed questions. It is not a random sample of GSM8K or either complete resource. A seeded SHA256 priority order randomized ordering only within this restricted pool.

Both reviewers assessed all 50 families, four variants each, by independently deriving answers and checking the requested transformations. **30 families passed both first reviews; 20 failed the strict admission rule because at least one review was reject or uncertain.** The review-pair counts were: 30 pass/pass, 6 reject/reject, 7 uncertain/uncertain, 5 uncertain/pass, 1 pass/uncertain and 1 uncertain/reject. An admission rejection can concern construct fit or unresolved wording, so neither 20 exclusions nor 6 double rejections means that many proven wrong-gold families.

The original exact-32-family gate therefore **failed**. Its failure remains in [`source_inputs/GATE32_FAILED.json`](source_inputs/GATE32_FAILED.json) and the original preparation folder. A **separate 30-family cohort** uses all strict double-pass families in the existing priority order, with 15 selection and 15 confirmation families. No uncertain judgment was overridden; no question or gold answer was edited. See [`selection_plan30.json`](selection_plan30.json), [`source_disposition.json`](source_disposition.json) and [`SOURCE30_VALIDATION.json`](SOURCE30_VALIDATION.json).

## Other exclusion classes and limits

Other findings are distinct from these arithmetic contradictions: R050's paraphrase changes a character dependency and becomes underdetermined; R011's numerical change introduces an unmatched promotion case; R037 has an unstated rounding convention; and R032 needs an unstated link between wind speed and fog movement. Some cases involve ambiguous comparisons, reference periods, usable versus unoccupied capacity, or discrete rounding. First-review disagreements remain visible; this note does not promote every ambiguity into a proven source error.

Reproduction requires no model calls: fetch the exact pinned JSONL, verify its full-file digest, select the three zero-based rows, verify their row digests and compare the source final answers with the displayed arithmetic and the two first reviews. The complete 50-family disposition and the strict double-pass selection can be regenerated by `prepare_source30.py` from the preserved source preparation and four review files.

These checks are **independent agent source reviews, not external human certification**. Shared-model mistakes remain possible. The three witnesses establish specific inconsistencies at these source pins; they do not establish their novelty, prevalence, or presence in future dataset versions. Source authors were not contacted. This note reports no solver-performance result.

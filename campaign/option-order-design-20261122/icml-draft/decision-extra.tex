\section{Frozen decision experiment}\label{app:frozen-decision}
On 288 ARC-Challenge and 288 OpenBookQA items, we compared Qwen2.5
1.5B Instruct with Granite 3.3 2B Instruct (Q4\_K\_M) under a
standard numeric-choice prompt. The frozen decision was whether
Qwen led by at least five percentage points. The reference used all
24 orders per model and question. We convolved the exact distributions
of the independently sampled four-order blocks and used 4,000 paired
item bootstrap draws for simultaneous directional bounds across panels.

On ARC-Challenge, the full-order lead was 2.894 points and Williams
reduced decision error from 1.197\% to 1.172\% versus precedence
minimax. Ordinary cyclic yielded 1.471\%. The Williams reduction was
0.025 points, with directional bounds $[-0.255, 0.417]$ points.
OpenBookQA's lead was $-7.002$ points, 12.002 points from the
threshold, and all three decision errors were effectively zero.
The frozen comparison excluded a reduction above one point on both
panels. Only ARC-Challenge was relatively close to the threshold.
